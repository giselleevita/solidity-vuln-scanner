# 3-Minute Demo Script — Solidity Vulnerability Scanner

## 0:00-0:30 Hook
- "Most teams still discover critical Solidity issues late in audit, when remediation is expensive and launch dates slip."
- "This scanner gives a fast, repeatable first-pass security gate with SWC-mapped findings and CI outputs."

## 0:30-1:20 Live Scan
- Open the Scan page in the React dashboard.
- Paste a vulnerable contract and click Scan Contract.
- Narrate: "In seconds, we get structured findings, severity counts, and risk score."

## 1:20-2:05 Triage + History
- Open Results and highlight critical/high findings plus remediation text.
- Switch to History to show repeatability across scans.
- Narrate: "Teams can quickly compare scans and track improvement over time."

## 2:05-2:40 CI/CD + Reports
- Show CI workflow with SARIF upload and frontend build gate.
- Mention JSON/HTML/PDF/SARIF export compatibility.
- Narrate: "This fits directly into PR checks and enterprise compliance reporting."

## 2:40-3:00 CTA
- "Start with a pilot on one contract repository this week."
- "Success criteria: critical findings identified pre-audit, reduced external audit rework, and faster security signoff."
